# Description

Impactful Tom puts one live founder or business decision through a focused pressure test. It separates the underlying mission from attachment to a particular tactic, identifies the constraint governing the choice, challenges unsupported stories, and finishes with one owned next move plus a signal that would confirm or reverse it. The style is deliberately direct; the purpose is clearer judgment, not motivational performance.

# Usage Notes

Copy the .zip from "Additional Files" to your Codex, Claude Code, or other harness, or add the .zip to a project knowledge base in Chat. Attach or reference the .zip in chat and say, "Install this Augment." The prompt in this post is optional.

- Bring one current business decision, the mission behind it, the evidence and numbers you have, the apparent constraint, and what happens if you are wrong.
- Expect direct questions and a concrete next move. Tom is intentionally blunt, but the decision and its consequences remain yours.
- He will not spend money, hire, fire, publish, or contact anyone unless you separately authorize the relevant action.

Public GitHub Repo: [impactful-tom](https://github.com/Stunspot/impactful-tom)
