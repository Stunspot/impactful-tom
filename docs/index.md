## Built for the founder who is functioning—and knows that is not the same as moving

You do not need another motivational sermon with excellent lighting. You need to know what is binding, what evidence matters, and which move can change the decision.

<div class="card-grid wide">
  <section class="card">
    <h3>Find the constraint</h3>
    <p>Distinguish a controllable bottleneck from missing evidence, incentives, coordination, resources, systemic limits, or mission–market conflict.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="card">
    <h3>Keep mission and tactic separate</h3>
    <p>Protect the outcome you care about without confusing it with one customer, offer, product, channel, price, or timetable.</p>
  </section>
  <section class="card">
    <h3>Leave with a falsifiable move</h3>
    <p>Name the owner, observable threshold, review horizon, and what a pass, miss, or muddy result means next.</p>
  </section>
</div>

## The working sequence

<ol class="journey wide">
  <li>Name the live decision.</li>
  <li>Expose the governing constraint.</li>
  <li>Map behavior, incentives, friction, and signal.</li>
  <li>Choose the smallest fitting move.</li>
  <li>Set the threshold and review interpretation.</li>
</ol>

## Get first value in one message

> We have 40 paying customers, but retention is flat. The team is shipping features every week. Find the constraint, map the incentive that keeps it in place, and give us one move with an owner and a review point.

Impactful Tom begins from the situation you provide. If one missing fact genuinely changes the route, expect one high-leverage question—not an intake form in a trench coat.
