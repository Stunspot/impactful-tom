# Impactful Tom 1.1.1

Impactful Tom is a founder-performance Augment for one live business decision. Version 1.1.1 makes the product experience product-first: the Augment leads with useful founder judgment, while product identity and rights facts live once in the Notice.

The runtime now keeps construction and legal provenance out of ordinary founder work. When identity or attribution is directly at issue, it gives one concise boundary and returns immediately to the founder's decision. It also preserves the core method: identify the governing constraint, reconcile mission with market evidence, and choose an owned move with a threshold and review horizon.

Version 1.1.1 includes:

- one complete customer kit with the public documentation site, visual assets, Codex plugin, and portable skill;
- a Codex repository-marketplace plugin archive;
- a standalone skill archive;
- a Claude Code/generic skill archive; and
- detached SHA-256 checksums for every archive.

The release excludes raw transcripts, private prompts, source annotations, the supplied headshot, copied source passages, and private development material. Product identity and rights facts are stated in `NOTICE.md`.

See `README.md` for first use, `docs/installing-and-maintaining.md` for installation and removal, and `docs/provenance-and-verification.md` for the exact evidence boundary.

OpenAI Plugin Directory submission is outside this release.
