# Founder Move

## Governing decision

Move standard proposal ownership from the founder to the senior seller, with founder review reserved for defined enterprise exceptions.

## Diagnosed constraint

A coordination and decision-right bottleneck: the founder is acting as the approval queue for work the senior seller can handle.

## Evidence posture

- Reported: founder-written proposals delay responses by five days; a senior seller can handle standard deals; enterprise exceptions are rare.
- Observed: none.
- Inferred: founder authorship, not proposal complexity, is the binding constraint.
- Current posture: provisional.

## Move

Give the senior seller authority to send standard proposals using a defined template and exception checklist; route only exceptions to the founder.

- Owner: Senior seller.
- Observable threshold: 90% of standard-deal proposals are sent within one business day for two weeks.
- Review date or horizon: Two weeks.
- Guardrail: Founder review remains mandatory for exceptions defined in the checklist.

## Predeclared interpretation

- If the threshold is met: make seller-owned standard proposals the default.
- If it is missed: identify whether the failure is qualification, template, pricing authority, or exception criteria; fix that constraint without restoring blanket founder authorship.
- If the result is ambiguous: review the delayed proposals and revise the exception checklist.

## Open assumption

The senior seller can reliably distinguish a standard deal from a true enterprise exception.
