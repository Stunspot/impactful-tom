Don’t buy more leads yet. Your reported funnel closes about 7–8 new customers monthly; six flat months means the binding constraint is unresolved retention/onboarding economics, not automatically top-of-funnel.

For the next 10 business days, assign one owner to cohort every customer closed in the past six months: segment, promised outcome, first-value date, onboarding milestones, 30/60/90-day use, expansion/contraction, and churn reason. Test whether accounts that miss first value by day 18 churn or contract at least twice as often as those that reach it.

If they do, freeze the lead push and redesign onboarding around faster first value for that segment. If they do not, inspect the same cohort for ICP/offer mismatch or contraction before spending on acquisition. Don’t use discounts or manual heroics to make onboarding look better; that just buries the constraint.
