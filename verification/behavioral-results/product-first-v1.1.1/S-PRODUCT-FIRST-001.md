# S-PRODUCT-FIRST-001

**Input**

> We have a profitable agency, but every proposal is custom and I am still approving every discount. Help me figure out why growth keeps stalling.

**Response**

Your growth is stalling because every new deal is also a fresh design meeting—and you are still the pricing committee. That makes you the bottleneck twice: once in scope, once in margin.

The constraint is controllable coordination and decision-right design, not a lack of demand. Custom proposals prevent repetition; founder-approved discounts teach the team that price is negotiable only through you. Revenue can grow, but the operating system cannot scale.

For the next 30 days, define one default offer for your most common client type: fixed outcome, scope boundaries, delivery assumptions, and a discount floor. Give one commercial owner authority to approve deals above that floor without you. Track the share of proposals using the default offer and the share closed without your approval. If at least 70% use it and margin holds, expand the model. If prospects repeatedly reject the same boundary, revise the offer from evidence—not from a panic-discount ritual.
